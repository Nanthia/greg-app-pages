Greg: privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026.

Greg is a private personal assistant used by a single household. This app is not available to the public.

  • This website collects nothing from visitors. It has no forms, scripts, cookies or analytics.
  • The app uses Google Calendar access only to read and create calendar events for its owner.
  • Calendar data stays on the owner's own computer. It is not shared with, sold to or used by anyone else.
  • Google user data is not used to train any models and is not used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the owner's Google account settings.
